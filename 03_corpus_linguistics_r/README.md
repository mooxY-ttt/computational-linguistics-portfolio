# Corpus Linguistics and Text Mining (R)

## Overview
This project applies tidy data principles to corpus linguistics for quantitative literary analysis, specifically focusing on Jane Austen's *Pride and Prejudice*.

## Key Visualization
![Top 15 Frequent Words in Pride and Prejudice](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/efba1081-7606-49cf-998c-7a500abf0574)

## Linguistic Observations
The frequency distribution highlights the narrative focus of the novel. Words like "elizabeth", "darcy", "miss", and "time" dominate the text, reflecting the character-driven and social nature of Austen's writing.

## Technical Details
- **Tech Stack**: R, `tidyverse`, `tidytext`, `ggplot2`.
- **Methodology**: Utilizes the `tidytext` framework to tokenize text, filters stop words, and generates publication-quality visualizations.
